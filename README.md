# Superstore Marketing Campaign Analysis

## Business Problem
A superstore chain runs a year-end membership campaign offering 20% discount on future purchases. The goal is to understand whether the campaign is effective and how future targeting can be improved.

## Objective
- Analyze customer behavior
- Segment customers
- Identify drivers of campaign response
- Predict likely membership acceptance
- Recommend business actions

## Key Insights
- Overall response rate: 14.91%
- Responders spent more than non-responders
- Catalog and web-engaged customers showed higher response
- High-income and high-value customers were stronger target segments
- Top 20% model-scored customers achieved 53.93% response rate
- Targeting lift: 3.61x

## Tools Used
Python, Pandas, Seaborn, Scikit-learn, Power BI

## Business Recommendation
Instead of targeting all customers equally, the company should prioritize recent, high-value, and catalog/web-engaged customers to improve campaign ROI.
